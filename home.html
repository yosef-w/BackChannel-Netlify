Questions

Is BackChannel free?

Yes, you can download it and use it for free.

Do I need to know someone at the company?

No. That's the point. You match with employees who opted in to referring people like you.

Who sees my résumé?

Your résumé is shared with a sponsor only after the two of you match. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Does a referral guarantee an interview?

No. A referral gets you noticed, and the company decides the rest.
