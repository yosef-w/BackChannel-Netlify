1. Overview

BackChannel is a mobile app, operated by Bluejay Labs LLC ("BackChannel," "we," "us," or "our"), that connects job seekers ("Applicants") with employees at companies ("Sponsors") who are willing to refer candidates for open roles. Applicants build a profile and browse sponsored jobs; Sponsors review Applicant profiles and decide whether to refer someone they're interested in. When both sides express interest, a match opens a chat, and a match can lead to a referral that we track through the stages of a hiring pipeline.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you use BackChannel, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights available to you — including how to delete your account and your data. We've written it to describe what the app actually does today; if a future feature changes any of this, we'll update the policy first.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create an account and build your profile, you provide information directly. For every user, this includes:

  • Your name and email address, and a password if you sign up with email rather than with Apple or Google
  • Your city and state or region, which you can enter using an address-autocomplete field. We collect only the city and state — not a street address or postal code
  • A profile photo, if you add one
  • A portfolio URL, if you add one
  • A short bio

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive Apple's private relay address instead of your real email, and that relay address becomes your account email.

If you use BackChannel as an Applicant, we also collect:

  • Your target industry, desired roles, and skills
  • Work authorization status, willingness to relocate, and any visa or sponsorship needs
  • Achievements you choose to highlight
  • Professional experience — job titles, companies, employment dates, and descriptions
  • Education, certifications, and languages
  • Short answers to prompts ("insights") that help Sponsors get a sense of who you are
  • Your uploaded resume file, the text we extract from it, and the fields our systems classify from that text (for example, skills, job titles, or dates) so your profile is easier to search and match against open roles

If you use BackChannel as a Sponsor, we also collect:

  • Your company and job title
  • Your work email address, which we use to verify that you control an email account at the company you say you work for. Verifying it is optional, but some Sponsor features require it
  • Your tenure at the company
  • Your referral preferences and the companies you're able to refer candidates into
  • Short answers to "insights" prompts
  • Job postings you create or sponsor — the title, description, and any "insider insights" you write. If you import a posting from a URL, we also collect that URL and the content of the page it points to

Information from your activity

As you use the app, we collect information about your activity, including:

  • Jobs you like or pass on, and profiles you like
  • Matches you form with other users
  • Messages you send and receive in the app
  • Referrals you make or receive, the "vouch" statement a Sponsor writes when submitting one, and status updates as a referral moves through a hiring pipeline
  • Entries you submit to a waitlist, if you join one
  • Reports you submit about another user — the person reported, the reason you select, and any details you add — and the blocks that result (see Section 7)

Information collected automatically

We also collect certain information automatically when you use the app:

  • A push-notification device token and platform, so we can deliver notifications to your device. You control which notifications you receive in the app's Notification settings
  • Product-analytics events, such as which screens you view and which actions you take, and a device identifier that lets our analytics tool recognize the same device across sessions. Your analytics profile is also associated with your account details — name, email, company, job title, city, role, and whether your email is verified — so we can understand how different kinds of users experience the app. Analytics events do not include the contents of your messages, resume, or search terms
  • Crash and diagnostic data when something in the app doesn't work as expected. This is associated with your account ID so we can follow up on a problem you hit. It does not include screenshots of your screen or the contents of your messages
  • Standard device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version

3. What we don't collect

To be clear about the limits: BackChannel does not ask for or collect your phone number, date of birth, street address, or postal code. The app does not access your device's precise location (GPS), your contacts, your photo library beyond the specific photo or file you choose to upload, or your microphone. We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the app — create and maintain your account, build your profile, and let you browse jobs or applicants
  • Power matching between Applicants and Sponsors
  • Facilitate referrals and track them through the hiring pipeline
  • Enable messaging between users who've matched
  • Process resumes and job postings with AI so profiles and listings are accurate and searchable
  • Send you notifications, such as a new match, a new message, a referral status update, or a reminder that a fresh set of profiles or roles is ready — each of which you can turn off in the app's Notification settings
  • Analyze how the app is used so we can fix problems and build better features
  • Review reports, enforce our Terms of Service, and detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. How your information is shared

Sharing information with other users is a core part of how BackChannel works, not a side effect. Specifically:

  • If you're an Applicant, Sponsors at companies with open roles can see your profile — your name, photo, city, bio, skills, experience, education, and "insights" answers — when deciding whether to express interest in you. Your resume file is shared with a Sponsor once you and that Sponsor have matched, because reviewing it is the point of a referral introduction.
  • If you're a Sponsor, Applicants can see the information you've chosen to share on your profile — your name, photo, job title, company, tenure, and "insights" answers — alongside the roles you sponsor. Your work email address and your login email are never shown to other users.
  • Messages you send within a match are visible to the other person in that match.
  • Referral and pipeline status you or the other party update is visible to both people involved in that referral.
  • Profile photos are served from a content-delivery network; the links aren't published anywhere, but anyone who has a photo's exact link can view it.

Beyond sharing between users, we share information:

  • With the service providers described in Section 4, only to the extent needed for them to perform their function
  • If required by law, legal process, or government request, or where we believe it's necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of BackChannel, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of company assets, in which case your information would remain subject to a policy that protects it

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Reporting, blocking, and safety

You can report another user from their profile, from a job posting, or from a conversation. When you report someone, we record who you reported, the reason you selected, and any details you added, and a member of our team reviews it. Reporting also blocks the other person immediately: you will no longer be shown to each other anywhere in the app, any match or conversation between you is closed, and any pending referral between you is withdrawn. Blocks are permanent and can't be undone from within the app.

We keep report records for as long as they're needed to review the report, enforce our Terms, and keep the platform safe, including after either account is deleted where necessary to prevent abuse. The person you report is not told who reported them.

8. Sensitive information

Some of what you share with us is sensitive — most of all your resume and work history, which can reveal a great deal about your background, and your work-authorization and visa status if you provide them. We collect this information because it's necessary for the core features of the app: matching you to relevant roles and giving a Sponsor what they need to decide whether to refer you. We limit how it's shared to the uses described in this policy — for example, showing your resume to a Sponsor you've matched with, because that's the purpose of a referral introduction.

Our matching and feed ordering are algorithmic, but we do not use your information to make fully automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Whether you receive a referral, an interview, or an offer is decided by people — Sponsors and employers — not by BackChannel.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, so the app continues to work as you'd expect — including your match history, past messages, and referral status.

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account record, profile, resume and extracted resume text, photos, likes, matches, conversations and messages, referrals, waitlist entries, and device tokens from our systems; remove your uploaded files from storage; and, if you signed in with Apple, revoke the sign-in authorization with Apple. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. Residual copies in routine system backups are overwritten in the ordinary course of operations. We may retain limited records where we're required to for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention purposes — for example, a report you submitted or that was submitted about you — and even then only for as long as necessary.

Aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer reasonably be linked to you may be retained without a fixed time limit.

10. Your rights and choices

Access. Most of the information we hold about you is visible directly in the app, on your profile. If you'd like a copy of the information we hold about you, email us at the address in Section 15 and we'll provide it.

Correct. You can update or correct your profile information at any time using the profile editing screens in the app.

Notifications. You can turn individual notification types on or off in the app's Notification settings, and you can disable notifications entirely in your device's system settings.

Delete. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. If you signed up with Apple or Google and haven't set a password, the app will first email you a link to set one, since deletion requires confirming your identity. Once you confirm, deletion happens as described in Section 9.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law — for example, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to receive your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We won't discriminate against you for exercising them.

11. Data security

We use measures designed to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit, storing passwords only in hashed form so they are never kept as plain text, storing resume files privately so they're only served to people the app is showing them to, and restricting internal access to what's needed to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account and to contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

12. Children's privacy

BackChannel is not directed to children. The Service is intended for people who are at least 16 years old and legally able to seek employment or refer candidates, and by creating an account you represent that you meet that requirement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has created an account, we will delete that account and the associated information.

13. International data transfers

Our infrastructure and service providers are located primarily in the United States. If you access BackChannel from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and potentially other countries, where data protection laws may differ from those in your home country. By using BackChannel, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BackChannel evolves. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and if a change is material, we'll provide additional notice — for example, through an in-app notification — before it takes effect.

15. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, or want to exercise any of the rights described above, contact us at support@backchannel.app.
