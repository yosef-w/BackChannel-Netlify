1. Overview

BackChannel is a mobile app that connects job seekers ("Applicants") with employees at companies ("Sponsors") who are willing to refer candidates for open roles. Applicants build a profile and browse sponsored jobs; Sponsors review applicant profiles and decide whether to refer someone they're interested in. When both sides express interest, a match opens a chat, and a match can lead to a referral that we track through stages of a hiring pipeline.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you use BackChannel, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights available to you — including how to delete your account and your data.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create an account and build your profile, you provide us with information directly. This includes, for every user:

  • Name, email address, password, and username
  • Phone number, including country code
  • Date of birth
  • Location or address, including street, city, state, zip or postal code, and country
  • A profile photo
  • A portfolio URL, if you add one
  • A short bio

If you use BackChannel as an Applicant, we also collect:

  • Your target industry, desired roles, and skills
  • Work authorization status, willingness to relocate, and any visa or sponsorship needs
  • Achievements you choose to highlight
  • Professional experience — job titles, companies, employment dates, and descriptions
  • Education, certifications, and languages
  • Short answers to prompts ("insights") that help Sponsors get a sense of who you are
  • Your uploaded resume file, the text we extract from it, and fields our systems classify from that text (for example, skills, job titles, or dates) so your profile is easier to search and match against open roles

If you use BackChannel as a Sponsor, we also collect:

  • Your company and job title
  • Your work email address, which we use to verify that you control an email account at the company you say you work for
  • Your tenure at the company
  • Your referral preferences and the companies you're able to refer candidates into
  • Short answers to "insights" prompts

Information from your activity

As you use the app, we collect information about your activity, including:

  • Jobs you like or pass on, and profiles you like
  • Matches you form with other users
  • Messages you send and receive in the app
  • Referrals you make or receive, and status updates as a referral moves through a hiring pipeline
  • Entries you submit to a waitlist, if you join one

Information collected automatically

We also collect certain information automatically when you use the app:

  • A push-notification device token and platform, so we can deliver notifications to your device
  • Product-analytics events, such as which screens you view and which actions you take
  • Crash and diagnostic data when something in the app doesn't work as expected
  • Standard device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version

4. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the app — create and maintain your account, build your profile, and let you browse jobs or applicants
  • Power matching between Applicants and Sponsors
  • Facilitate referrals and track them through the hiring pipeline
  • Enable messaging between users who've matched
  • Process resumes and job postings with AI so profiles and listings are accurate and searchable
  • Send you notifications, such as a new match, a new message, or a referral status update
  • Analyze how the app is used so we can fix problems and build better features
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

5. How your information is shared

Sharing information with other users is a core part of how BackChannel works, not a side effect. Specifically:

  • When you and another user express mutual interest and form a match, the Sponsor in that match can see the Applicant's profile and the resume they've uploaded, and the Applicant can see the profile information the Sponsor has chosen to share.
  • Messages you send within a match are visible to the other person in that match.
  • Referral and pipeline status you or the other party update is visible to both people involved in that referral.

Beyond sharing between matched users, we share information:

  • With the service providers described in Section 3, only to the extent needed for them to perform their function
  • If required by law, legal process, or government request, or where we believe it's necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of BackChannel, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of company assets, in which case your information would remain subject to a policy that protects it

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Sensitive information

Some of what you share with us is sensitive: your resume (which can reveal a great deal about your work history and background), your date of birth, and your address or location. We collect this information because it's necessary for core features of the app — matching you to relevant roles, confirming you meet our minimum age requirement, and enabling location-aware search — and we limit how it's shared to the uses described in this policy, such as showing your resume to a Sponsor you've matched with because that's the purpose of a referral introduction. We do not use sensitive information to make fully automated decisions that have a significant effect on you without a person involved.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, so the app continues to work as you'd expect — including your match history, past messages, and referral status. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your information so it can no longer identify you, except where we're required to retain certain records for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, and even then only for as long as necessary. Aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer reasonably be linked to you may be retained without a fixed time limit.

8. Your rights and choices

Access. Most of the information we hold about you is visible directly in the app, on your profile.

Correct. You can update or correct your profile information at any time using the profile editing screens in the app.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time using the in-app Delete Account option in Settings. Once you confirm, we begin deleting or anonymizing your account data — including your profile, resume, photos, and messages — subject to the limited retention described in Section 7 above (for example, where we're required to keep certain records for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention reasons).

Account deletion can't be undone. Once it's processed, we can't recover your account or restore your data. If you'd rather not delete your account but want to ask us about the information we hold on you, email us at [Contact email].

9. Data security

We use measures designed to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit, restricting internal access to what's needed to operate the service, and storing passwords in hashed form so they're never kept as plain text. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account and to contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Children's privacy

BackChannel is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. The Service is intended for people who are at least 16 years old and legally able to seek employment or refer candidates. If we learn that someone under the applicable minimum age has created an account, we will take steps to delete that account and the associated information.

11. International data transfers

Our infrastructure and service providers are primarily located in the United States. If you access BackChannel from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and potentially other countries, where data protection laws may differ from those in your home country. By using BackChannel, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations.
